## Translation Evaluation

For each candidate, record source URLs and passage-level evidence for:

1. Textual transparency and consistency.
2. Preservation of ambiguity where the source text is ambiguous.
3. Treatment of gendered, difficult, culturally distant, and theologically significant language.
4. Textual basis and variant handling.
5. License, attribution, and redistribution rights.

Do not choose a default translation until its evaluation is complete.
